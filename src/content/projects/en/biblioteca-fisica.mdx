## 🙋🏻‍♂️ My role in the project

I played an integral role as a frontend programmer and UX/UI designer, participating in the following areas:

- **User interface design**: I collaborated in creating mockups and prototypes using Figma, focusing on designing an intuitive, easy-to-use, and visually appealing interface. I worked on elements such as menu layout, buttons, and navigation flows to ensure a smooth user experience.
- **Frontend programming**: I developed the graphical interfaces and main components of the application using **React.js** for application behavior logic and **Styled Components** for styling. This allowed us to create a dynamic, responsive, and easy-to-maintain interface.

## ✨ Project importance

This project is important because it solves a common problem among students: the difficulty of efficiently accessing and managing library resources. The **Physics Library** not only simplifies the book search and reservation process but also encourages the use of available academic resources, contributing to the comprehensive training of students.

## 🎇 Impact on users

The application benefits students in the following ways:

- **Quick access to resources**: Allows students to view the books available in the library quickly and easily, without needing to go physically.
- **Efficient reservations**: Students can reserve books from anywhere and pick them up later, optimizing their time and avoiding queues or unnecessary waits.
- **Personalized profile**: Each student can manage their profile, view active reservations, and maintain a history of the books they have used.
- **Intuitive interface**: An easy-to-use and responsive interface ensures a positive experience for users, regardless of their technology skill level.
