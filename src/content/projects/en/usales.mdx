## 🙋🏻‍♂️ My role in the project
I was a fundamental part of the team, working as a full-stack developer and UX/UI designer. My responsibilities included:

- **Design and analysis**: I collaborated in defining the project scope and functional requirements, creating diagrams and technical documentation to guide development.
- **User interface design**: I was involved in designing screens, focusing on usability and user experience. I worked on details such as button placement, icons, and navigation flows to ensure an intuitive and pleasant interaction.
- **Application development**: I actively participated in programming both the frontend (creating visual components) and the backend (business logic and database management), ensuring the system was functional and scalable.

## ✨ Project importance
This project is important because it addresses a common problem in small businesses: the lack of affordable and easy-to-use tools to manage their operations. **Usales** not only simplifies administrative tasks but also provides valuable information for decision-making, which can make a difference in business profitability.

## 🎇 Business impact
**Usales** can help restaurants in the following ways:

- **Time savings**: Automates repetitive tasks such as sales recording and inventory control.
- **Error reduction**: Minimizes human errors in data management and financial calculations.
- **Scalability**: Designed to grow with the business, adapting to future needs.
